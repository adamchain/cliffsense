Why you are invited

Keeping benefits is not one task. It is notices, renewals, income changes, proof, deadlines, follow-up, and the handoff when another caregiver must step in. You see that burden every day, and your judgment can make Bene-Watch clearer, safer and more useful.

The need we will demo

Track deadlines, changes, proof and follow-up. Help prepare accurate, complete government forms to reduce omissions and missed steps that can interrupt benefits. Preserve a record for the next caregiver.

Who the pilot is for

Built with the people who carry the load.

Individuals & families

Join the beta. Bring the notices, deadlines and changes you manage today, and tell us what would make Bene-Watch worth trusting.

Managed care organizations

Use the Bene-Watch platform to help members keep benefits and reduce paperwork between renewals and care-management contacts.

Nonprofits & special-needs lawyers

Give the families you serve a continuing record that connects notices, proof, deadlines and open questions between engagements.

Public stakeholders

Explore better-prepared submissions, administrative efficiency and less avoidable churn.

What you can expect

A guided walkthrough of deadlines, changes, form preparation, the evidence vault, risk prompts, follow-up and caregiver succession.

What we ask from you

Tell us what feels clear, what feels incomplete or confusing, which real-life events are missing, and what would make you trust it for a family or partner cohort.

What participation should produce

Better workflows, clearer alerts, safer wording, stronger onboarding, and practical ways to measure whether Bene-Watch reduces missed steps and reconstruction work.

The difference we can make together

A continuing household record supports families and their partners. The pilot will test clearer forms, timely follow-up and less reconstruction work. These are goals to measure, not promised outcomes.

What the pilot will assess

Measured in Pennsylvania first.

An initial 15-family beta is underway. A scoped pilot with individuals and managed care organizations will look at four things.

Benefit retention
Renewals
Staff efficiency
Reductions in churn

Pilot measures are goals to evaluate, not promised outcomes. Results will be reported with their definitions and limits.

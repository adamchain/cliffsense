Bene-Watch is being shaped by the families and caregivers who manage benefits every day. Here are their stories.

Where Bene-Watch began · Frank & Graham

A father building the system he wished he’d always had.

For years, Frank Rapoport has managed his son Graham’s SSDI, SNAP and Medicaid benefits. Talking with other parents, he kept running into the same gap: lawyers and consultants step in to help secure benefits, or to fight for them after they’re lost. Almost no one helps families keep them in the first place.

Frank also thought about the day he won’t be the one keeping watch. Graham’s two brothers will eventually take over his care, and Frank didn’t want them digging through years of paperwork to piece together what happened and why.

Prevention, not rescue. Bene-Watch is built to help families stay ahead of renewals, notices and deadlines instead of scrambling after coverage is lost.
A record that outlasts him. The Vault keeps Graham’s benefit documents, past renewals and eligibility decisions in one place, so the next submission is easier and the family’s hard-won knowledge doesn’t live only in Frank’s head.
Room to grow. As Frank explores a raise or more work for Graham, Bene-Watch is designed to show how higher earnings could affect each benefit before a decision puts his coverage or financial security at risk.

Helen & Meghan · Philadelphia

Peace of mind for a mother who won’t always be there.

Helen’s daughter, Meghan, is a young adult in the Philadelphia Independence Network. As Helen gets older, one question follows her everywhere: what happens to Meghan if she isn’t around to keep track of it all?

For parents like Helen, the worry never really switches off. One missed notice or lapsed renewal could put a child’s Medicaid or Medicare coverage at risk, and the fear of that phone call or letter is constant.

Bene-Watch gives Helen a way to watch over Meghan’s coverage now, with deadlines, documents and notices in one place, so a missed step is far less likely to put Meghan’s health care at risk. It protects Meghan, and it gives Helen something she hasn’t had in a long time: peace of mind.

Why it matters

An eligibility system processes the case. Families still need help completing and documenting everything required to keep that case current.

Between January 2025 and June 2026, Pennsylvania DHS records show 523,711 Medical Assistance closure entries for missing required information and 57,473 SNAP income-test closure entries.

Recorded closure actions, not unique people; they do not show why information was missing or that every closure was wrong.

Where Bene-Watch fits

The gap is between agency transactions.

Bene-Watch is designed to work beside official systems, not replace them.

Government agencies

  • Determine eligibility
  • Issue official notices
  • Accept and decide filings
+

Household continuity with Bene-Watch

  • Track what is due
  • Organize proof and receipts
  • Follow unresolved actions

Official decisions stay with agencies. The household keeps a clearer working record.

Who we partner with

Value for every organization that supports families.

Medicaid MCOs & CHC plans

Support members between renewals and care-management contacts, with a household record that keeps deadlines, proof and follow-up in view.

Health systems & providers

Fewer avoidable coverage interruptions that disrupt care and create reconnection work for your teams.

Nonprofits & special-needs lawyers

Connect notice and category evidence with requested proof, separate deadlines, receipts and open questions, between counseling sessions and legal engagements.

Foundations & disability organizations

Fund and help design pilots that test whether a continuing household record improves follow-up for the families you serve.

Caregiving organizations & counties

Help families and the people who support them keep one organized record, and hand it off without starting from scratch.

Government & elected officials

Complementary household support: better-prepared submissions, clearer records and more timely responses.

Commonwealth agencies decide eligibility

These are benefits we plan to test with partners. Sharing a family’s information always requires that family’s permission.
